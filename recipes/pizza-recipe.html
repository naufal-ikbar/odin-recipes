Delicious Pizza Recipes

Description

A delicious homemade pizza with a crispy crust, rich tomato sauce, melted cheese, and tasty toppings. It is simple to make and perfect for a family meal or a casual dinner.

Ingredients

  • 250 g all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon instant yeast
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 150 ml warm water
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 4 tablespoons tomato sauce
  • 150 g mozzarella cheese
  • 50 g pepperoni or sausage
  • ½ onion, sliced
  • ½ bell pepper, sliced

Steps

  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar, and salt in a bowl.
  2. Add the warm water and olive oil, then mix until the dough forms.
  3. Knead the dough for about 5–10 minutes until it becomes smooth.
  4. Cover the dough and let it rest for about 1 hour.
  5. Roll the dough into a round pizza shape.
  6. Spread the tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  7. Add mozzarella cheese, pepperoni or sausage, onion, and bell pepper.
  8. Bake the pizza at 220°C (425°F) for about 12–15 minutes.
  9. Remove from the oven, slice, and serve while hot.
