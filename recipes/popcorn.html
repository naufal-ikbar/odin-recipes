Delicious Popcorn Recipes

Description

A simple and delicious homemade popcorn that is crispy, buttery, and slightly salty. It is a perfect snack for watching movies or relaxing at home.

Ingredients

  • ½ cup popcorn kernels
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter
  • ½ teaspoon salt

Steps

  1. Heat the cooking oil in a large pot over medium heat.
  2. Add the popcorn kernels and cover the pot with a lid.
  3. Shake the pot gently every few seconds to prevent the kernels from burning.
  4. Wait until the kernels begin to pop.
  5. Continue cooking until the popping sounds become less frequent.
  6. Turn off the heat and carefully remove the lid.
  7. Pour the melted butter over the popcorn.
  8. Add the salt and mix well.
  9. Serve immediately and enjoy!
